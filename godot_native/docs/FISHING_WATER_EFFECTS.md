# Bounded native fishing water

Water is a presentation consumer of the existing rhythm model and surfaced-fish geometry. It never dispatches controls, scores a note, grants a reward or writes a save.

The valid-cast bite gets one small ripple. A fish thrust throws local spray only after the real retrieval depth approaches the surface, at most once per phrase. Successful late retrieval and successful completion produce their own short spray. Misses and failed completion never manufacture a catch. Local spray originates at the displayed fish/float projection, and its strength reads the existing depth and tension.

At most three local bursts and four camera droplets exist. A local burst has seven analytic droplets and two expanding rings, lasts 0.72 seconds and uses no spawned particle nodes. Camera droplets hang briefly, slide down and fade within 1.12 seconds. One native CanvasItem shader refracts only those droplet shapes; it does not blur or wash the full screen. The complete future droplet path must fit a lake-only band clear of the header, rhythm and tension panels. Ending dialogs are excluded too. Camera art ignores all input.

Pause freezes event latches and effect ages, and hides the lens over the pause dialog. Resume continues the same ages. Failure clears the pools; retry clears pools, clocks and event latches. Backwards model time also resets the consumer. Reattaching to an existing state does not replay historical events. Reduced motion removes camera droplets and converts new water cues to short stationary rings.

The focused test uses all four checked-in source input traces at 20 and 60 updates per second, checks unchanged model state, deduplicated events, hard pool bounds, expiration, failed/paused/retried flows and desktop/portrait/short-landscape exclusion geometry. Source trace replays and prepared state fixtures are not earned campaign progress or physical-device performance evidence.
