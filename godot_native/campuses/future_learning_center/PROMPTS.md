# Built-in imagegen reproduction specifications

These are consolidated generation/edit specifications, rather than a verbatim transcript of every exploratory prompt. All accepted images were made with the built-in imagegen tool; no API/CLI model fallback. Raw generated originals are retained. Reference roles: the supplied plan controls room adjacency and doorway side; the supplied interior photographs control materials; the accepted 3F art controls the shared oblique view and palette.

Shared specification: one isolated empty architectural room, transparent exterior, fine-detail JRPG pixel-art direction, near overhead orthographic south-facing cutaway, horizontal/vertical footprint axes, ivory plaster, dark slate structural trim, pale oak or pale stone floor, blue exterior glazing, warm daylight, restrained broad texture clusters. No movable furniture, equipment, people, text, labels, UI or floor plan symbols. Do not bake interactive objects into the base. Preserve actual alpha. Room masters must remain independent and use uniform placement scale.
